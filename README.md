# SaveBuddy
## SaveGuards - Acuin, Belida, Buaya, Cadenas, Cruda, Mallorca

### a. What problem are you trying to solve? Think about who has the problem, why it matters, and what people do about it today.
- Many people, especially students, struggle to track and budget their daily expenses. Nowadays, people would just simply rely on their memory or keep a piggy bank or old receipts that doesn't give an overview of their day-to-day spendings and isn't entirely reliable. This is why this matters: to prevent overspending that can quickly drain allowances before a week or a month ends.
  
### b. What are your app's features? How does the app help solve the problem?
- With SaveBuddy, a user can log their daily budget and expenditure by specifying a value, date and category. Once logged, these entries can be viewed, changed or removed via the app's calendar interface. SaveBuddy will then determine the user's expenditure and budget left over for any given day or month. This will allow them to better track their expenses, control their pocket money and prevent excessive spending. 

### c. What specific data will you handle?
SaveBuddy will keep the following information: daily target, list of expenses, name, cost, category and date. An ID will be allocated to each entry which will allow the user to search for, amend or remove an entry. SaveBuddy will then work out the sum of all expenses and any balance left. As mentioned above, all data will be written to a JSON file for persistence beyond the lifetime of the application.
